How Cashback Apps Work in Canada

A plain-English guide to Canadian cashback apps in 2026: how tracking works, CAD payouts, thresholds and what to check before you shop.

The short answer

Canadian cashback apps return a share of a purchase as points or credit when you shop through their links or scan a receipt. Availability varies by province and retailer, and rewards become redeemable only after a minimum threshold and pending period.

1. Two tracking methods

Online cashback starts when you click through the app or extension so the purchase is tracked by an affiliate link. Receipt apps work when you photograph a qualifying purchase. Some platforms offer both methods.

2. Canadian availability and CAD

Not all US cashback apps work in Canada. Confirm Canadian eligibility, CAD payouts or usable local gift cards, and which retailers are covered before you plan a purchase around an offer.

3. Thresholds and pending periods

Rewards usually sit as pending until the retailer confirms the order, which can take weeks. A minimum balance is often required before redemption, and returns typically void the cashback.

4. Practical cautions

  • Ad blockers and private browsing can break tracking
  • Rates may be capped or limited to selected items
  • Read the fine print before stacking coupons or cards

Common questions

Do cashback apps work in Canada?

Many do, but check Canadian eligibility, CAD payouts and the retailer list in the terms.

When can I cash out?

After you pass the minimum threshold and the pending confirmation window ends.

Do returns affect cashback?

Yes. Returned or cancelled orders are usually deducted from pending or earned rewards.

Are cashback apps safe?

Legitimate ones are, but verify reviews and terms and never pay a fee to release earnings.
