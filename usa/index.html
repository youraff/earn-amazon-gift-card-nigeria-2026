How to Get a Free Amazon Gift Card in the USA

A clear look at the legitimate ways US residents can earn Amazon gift card credit in 2026, and the scams to avoid.

The short answer

US residents can earn Amazon gift cards through receipt-scanning apps, cashback portals, research studies, trade-in programs and reward platforms. All are free to start, but none will pay a large card for almost no effort.

1. Receipt scanning and cashback

Upload ordinary grocery or shopping receipts to rewards apps and earn points that can be redeemed for Amazon credit. It is slow but steady, and it costs nothing to try.

  • Free to join
  • Check US eligibility
  • Redeem at a low minimum

2. Research studies and user testing

US-based research platforms sometimes pay well for focus groups and product tests. Availability depends on your profile and location, so treat this as an occasional bonus rather than a daily routine.

3. Amazon Trade-In

Send in eligible used electronics, books or devices and receive Amazon gift card credit once they are accepted. This is one of the most reliable routes because it is run by Amazon itself.

4. Reward and gaming apps

Some apps pay points for playing games or completing offers. Read the terms carefully so you know what each task requires and how long verification takes.

5. Stay safe

Never pay a fee to unlock a gift card, and ignore sites promising very large cards for almost no effort.

  • Avoid gift card generators
  • Never share bank details
  • Check official payout rules

Common questions

Do I need to live in the US?

Most US reward offers and Amazon.com gift cards are restricted to US residents. Check each platform eligibility rules before you spend time on it.

Are these methods really free?

Yes, legitimate reward platforms are free to join. If a site asks for an unlock fee or deposit, leave.

How long does it take?

It depends on the method and the card value. Receipt and cashback apps usually need a small minimum balance before redemption.

Can I use these cards outside the US?

Amazon gift cards are usually region locked. A US card normally works only on Amazon.com.
