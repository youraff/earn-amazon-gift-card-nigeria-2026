How Cashback and Reward Apps Work in the USA

A plain-English look at how US cashback apps calculate rewards, payout thresholds, returns and realistic expectations in 2026.

The short answer

Cashback apps earn you a percentage back when you shop through their links or scan receipts. Rewards are usually small, accumulate as points or credit, and become withdrawable only after a minimum threshold and verification.

1. Two common mechanisms

Online cashback works when you start your shopping trip from the app or extension, which tracks the purchase through an affiliate link. Receipt apps work by photographing a store receipt after a qualifying purchase. Some platforms use both.

2. What the rates really mean

Rates vary by store and category and are often higher for new customers. A headline rate may apply only to selected items or be capped at a maximum amount, so read the offer details before buying.

3. Thresholds and payout timelines

Most platforms require a minimum balance before you can cash out. Pending rewards can take weeks to confirm, and payout methods differ (gift card, bank credit, or statement credit).

4. Stacking and extensions

Some people combine a cashback portal with a rewards credit card or browser coupon tool. Terms differ, and stacking is not always allowed, so check each platformâ€™s rules.

5. Caveats worth knowing

  • Returns and cancellations usually void the cashback
  • Ad blockers or private browsing can break tracking
  • Caps and exclusions are common in fine print

Common questions

How do cashback apps make money?

They receive a commission from merchants for referred purchases and share part of it with you.

When can I withdraw my rewards?

Once you pass the minimum threshold and the pending period ends; timelines vary by platform.

Do returns affect cashback?

Yes. Returned or cancelled orders are typically deducted from pending or earned rewards.

Are cashback apps legitimate?

Many are, but check independent reviews and terms. Avoid any site that charges a fee to release earnings.
