How Reward Apps Work in the UK

A plain-English guide to how UK reward apps earn points, what those points are worth, payout thresholds and the fine print to check in 2026.

The short answer

Reward apps give you points or credit for everyday actions such as shopping through their links, answering surveys or scanning receipts. Points add up slowly, have a real cash value that varies by platform, and can usually be exchanged once you reach a minimum threshold.

1. The four common earning routes

Most UK reward apps use one or more of four methods: affiliate shopping (you start your purchase from the app), surveys (you answer questions for a small credit), receipt scanning (you photograph a qualifying purchase) and trial offers (you sign up for a service). Each has different effort and payout levels.

2. What points are really worth

Points are rarely worth one penny each. The value depends on how you redeem them — a gift voucher may give a better rate than cash. Before spending time on an app, look at its rewards catalogue and work out the effective value per point.

3. Thresholds and pending periods

You usually cannot withdraw straight away. Rewards are marked pending while the platform confirms the activity, then become available once you pass a minimum balance. Timelines vary, so read the payout terms before you start.

4. UK-specific things to check

  • Whether the app supports your bank or only vouchers
  • Whether rewards count as income for tax purposes
  • Whether the service is genuinely available in the UK, not just "global"
  • How the app handles returns, which often reverse the reward

5. Staying realistic

Reward apps are best viewed as a small discount on things you were already going to do, not a replacement income. Be wary of any service promising large guaranteed payouts for very little effort.

Common questions

What are reward apps in the UK?

Apps that give points or credit for shopping, surveys, scanning receipts or trying services, which can be exchanged for vouchers or cash.

How much are reward points worth?

Value varies by platform, often a fraction of a penny per point. Check the rewards catalogue to see the real exchange rate before investing time.

Do I need to pay tax on rewards in the UK?

Occasional small rewards are usually treated as hobby income, but rules depend on the activity. Check HMRC guidance or ask a tax adviser.

How long does a payout take?

After reaching the minimum threshold, pending rewards typically need verification and can take days or weeks depending on the platform.
