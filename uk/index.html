The short answer

In the UK you can earn vouchers through shopping cashback portals, receipt apps, mobile gaming rewards, walking apps and research panels. All are free to start and none require an upfront payment.

1. Shopping cashback and voucher sites

Several UK deal sites give gift cards when you shop eligible brands through their links. If you were going to buy anyway, this is the easiest route.

2. Walking and step apps

Step-tracking apps turn everyday walking into points. Payouts are usually small and may have daily caps or ad requirements, so read the rules.

3. Mobile gaming rewards

Some platforms pay points for reaching milestones in featured games. This can be faster than surveys but still takes consistent effort.

4. Passive and background apps

A few opt-in apps reward you for sharing spare bandwidth or usage data. Only install from known providers and check the privacy terms first.

5. Safety checklist

Legitimate UK voucher sites never ask for a fee or your bank details.

  • Never pay upfront
  • Check GBP rewards
  • Read payout rules
