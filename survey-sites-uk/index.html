How Survey Sites Work in the UK

An honest guide to UK survey and reward sites in 2026: eligibility, points, realistic earnings and how to spot unreliable platforms.

The short answer

UK survey sites pay small amounts in points or vouchers for opinions and tasks. Availability depends on your profile, screening-out is common, and earnings are modest. The biggest risk is time spent on panels that never pay.

1. UK eligibility and GBP payouts

Join only platforms that explicitly support the UK and explain how GBP cash or local vouchers work. Some sites are US-only and leave UK members with points they cannot redeem.

2. How surveys are scored

Reward points usually depend on survey length and how well you match the target demographic. You can be screened out partway through, so short, well-matched surveys are often better value than long ones.

3. Realistic earnings

This is spare-change money, not an income substitute. Treat surveys as an occasional activity and look for higher-value options like paid research or usability testing when available.

4. UK red flags

  • Promises of large rewards for almost no effort
  • Requests for upfront fees, ID scans or bank details
  • No clear payout terms or traceable company information
